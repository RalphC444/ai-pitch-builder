02 · Recommendations

What I'd change, and why

Each one stands on a usability reason today. The yellow tag marks where Mobbin references will be added as evidence once the connector is signed in.

Hierarchy & spacing

R1

Lead with the problem, not the score

Header becomes the issue in plain words ("Vague: add specifics from their screeners"), with the confidence % as a small chip. Show a one-line quote of the flagged statement.

Why: people act on "what's wrong", not on "76%". The popover covers the line it's about, so the quote keeps context without scrolling back.

Effort SMobbin: inline writing-assistant cards
R2

Make the rewrite the loudest text

Rewrite text at 14px in full ink; the strategy label ("Grounded in screener answer") drops to a small muted tag. Show the change as a diff: removed words struck, added words highlighted.

Why: the decision is "is this sentence better?"; a diff answers it at a glance instead of making you compare two paragraphs in your head.

Effort SMobbin: suggestion diffs / tracked changes
R3

One container, rows instead of boxes

Keep the popover's frame; separate suggestions with a hairline divider instead of bordered cards. Tighten padding from 32px to 18–20px; width from 600px to about 440px.

Why: nested borders make everything look equally important and eat roughly a third of the width. A narrower popover covers less of the pitch you're editing.

Effort SMobbin: compact popovers in editors
R4

Apply as a button, next to its rewrite, with Undo

A small primary "Apply" under each rewrite; after applying, the confirmation offers "Undo" alongside "Review next issue".

Why: a button reads as the action; placing it where your eye ends reading the rewrite shortens the path. Undo makes trying a suggestion safe, so people try more of them.

Effort SMobbin: apply/undo toasts

Sources & prompt flow

R5

Sources as numbered citations

Replace "3 Source(s) ▾" with small numbered markers ①②③ after each rewrite. Clicking one shows just that answer, with the supporting phrase highlighted, and a link to open it in Profile Data.

Why: keeps the popover a fixed size, and shows exactly which screener words back the claim, which is what a reviewer is checking. Your Figma's "Sources Indicators" frames point the same way.

Effort MMobbin: AI answers with citations
R6

Collapse "Prompt A Change" into a one-line ask

A single-line input at the bottom ("Ask AI to rewrite this…") plus quick chips: Shorter · More specific · Use their words. It expands only while typing.

Why: most fixes are one of a few common intents; chips get them in one click, and the free-text box stops competing with the suggestions for space.

Effort SMobbin: AI quick actions
R7

Keep existing suggestions while a prompt runs

New prompted results arrive as a new row on top with a loading line and inline Stop; the original suggestions stay underneath.

Why: people prompt to get something better than what's there; hiding the originals removes the comparison they need, and Stop then has nothing to return to.

Effort MMobbin: streaming AI responses
R8

Plain language throughout

"AI will use organic sources" becomes "Uses this advisor's screeners and profile". "Grounds Claim In Screener Answer" becomes "Uses their screener answer".

Why: RMs think in screeners and profiles, not "organic sources"; sentence-case labels read faster than Title Case.
